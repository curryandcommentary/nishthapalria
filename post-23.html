I Didn't Move to America for This Bharta (But Here We Are)

My weekday lunch is a negotiation with Saturday.

Ye hoon main. Nishtha. Nayi-nayi shaadi hui hai, writer hoon, aur is waqt apne kitchen mein khadi hoon, baingan ka bharta banate hue.

H4 life kuch aisi hai: ek insaan ghar se office ja raha hai, doosra ghar mein bharta bana raha hai. Dono ki apne-apne tareeke se lagi padi hai. Bas fark itna hai, ek ke paas laptop hai aur doosre ke paas pyaaz.

Mera weekday lunch khana nahi hai. Ye weekend ke saath ek negotiation hai.

Har Monday main khud ko samjhati hoon: ye kha lo, healthy hai, ghar ka hai. Bas Saturday tak survive kar jao.

Kyunki Saturday ko chaatar-matar thoosni hai.

Aaj meri plate mein jo bharta hai na, use dekh ke lagta hai isne zindagi mein bahut kuch dekha hai. Monday mornings dekhe hain. Unread emails dekhe hain. Taxes dekhe hain. Aur ab ye bas thak gaya hai.

Taste bura nahi hai. Main bura khana nahi banati. Meri lunch choices bas thodi dukhi hain.

Lekin Saturday tak pahunchna zaroori hai. Kyunki weekend par meri personality wapas aa jaati hai. Aur meri personality hai: pani puri, dahi puri, chaatar-patar.

Weekdays are for nutrition. Weekends are for character development.

Aaj ki plate meri asli cravings ka representation nahi hai. Agar hoti, toh saamne teekhe pani puri hote, uske baad chocolate shake, shayad fries bhi. Main calories count nahi kar rahi hoti. Main bas khush hoti.

Lekin adulthood mein hum apni cravings nahi khate. Hum baingan ka bharta khate hain. Aur Instagram par samosa dekh ke phone aise rakh dete hain jaise kisi ne personal insult kar diya ho.

H4 life ka ek aur interesting side effect hai. Jab aap ghar par kaafi waqt bitate ho, toh ghar ki cheezein dheere-dheere aapki dost ban jaati hain.

Ab mera kitchen mujhse baat karta hai. "Aao. Bhujia khuli hai. Thoda sa kha lo. Cheese toast bana lete hain? Fridge mein kuch meetha hai kya?"

Main likhna chahti hoon. Thoda padhna chahti hoon. Thode se time mein bahut kuch karna chahti hoon. Aur phir kitchen se awaaz aati hai: "Bhujia?"

Aur main sochti hoon… chalo, pehle bhujia.

Shayad is phase ka matlab yahi hai. Hum dono apni-apni jagah busy hain, apne-apne kaam mein lage hue hain, aur dono future ka intezar kar rahe hain.

Aur filhaal, mera future Saturday hai.

Saturday ko main bahar jaungi. Kuch accha khaungi. Kuch chaatar-patar karungi. Aur shayad life thodi normal lagegi.

Aur agar mera pati office mein ye padh raha hai, toh ek clarification: bhujia ka packet khula hua hai, lekin maine sirf thoda sa khaya hai. Aur "thoda" se mera matlab hai, packet mein jo hawa bachi hai na… woh maine nahi khayi.
